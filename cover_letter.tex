\subsubsection*{Context and Motivation}
In low-carbon power grids, the progressive displacement of synchronous thermal generation by inverter-based renewables severely degrades natural system inertia. Data centers house multi-megawatt electrochemical uninterruptible power supply (UPS) batteries that remain idle for over $99\%$ of their operating life. This manuscript introduces \textbf{PULSE} (\textbf{P}rimary-frequency \textbf{U}PS \textbf{L}ocal \textbf{S}ub-second \textbf{E}vent-triggered control), a communication-aware decentralized framework that mobilizes these distributed battery fleets to provide synthetic inertia and fast frequency support without requiring centralized dispatch.

\subsubsection*{Alignment with SEGAN Aims and Scope}
This work directly addresses core topics within the journal's scope:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Control of Distributed Energy Storage}: Decentralized coordination of distributed rack-level battery assets under strict local State-of-Charge (SoC) reserve constraints.
    \item \textbf{ICT Integration and Cyber-Physical Systems}: Event-triggered communication over shared local networks subject to packet loss, variable latencies, and jitter.
    \item \textbf{Grid Dynamics and Stability}: Rigorous frequency-domain delay margin formulations coupled with power-frequency swing dynamics.
    \item \textbf{Sustainability (UN SDGs 7 and 11)}: Unlocking pre-capitalized computing infrastructure for clean grid stabilization, displacing carbon-intensive fossil peaker reserves.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Core Contributions and Key Findings}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Zero-Communication Idle State \& 63.6\% Traffic Reduction}: PULSE employs an adaptive, time-relaxing event threshold that sends zero standby messages during normal ambient frequency drift, while cutting contingency communication traffic by $63.6\%$ compared to fixed-threshold schemes.
    \item \textbf{Realistic Measurement Filtering}: Evaluated under physical 1-cycle PLL ($65.0\,\mathrm{ms}$) and 2-cycle PMU ($90.5\,\mathrm{ms}$) low-pass filters under measurement noise, achieving smooth, non-saturating derivative-droop injection without inverter chattering.
    \item \textbf{Frequency Stabilization \& Inertia Support}: In a 300~MVA benchmark, 200 distributed agents ($12\,\mathrm{MW}$) arrest a $5\%$ generation trip at $59.721\,\mathrm{Hz}$ (vs. $59.423\,\mathrm{Hz}$ unsupported), providing a $+41.7\%$ virtual inertia boost and improving the 500~ms sliding-window RoCoF from $-0.441\,\mathrm{Hz/s}$ to $-0.285\,\mathrm{Hz/s}$.
    \item \textbf{Provable Delay Robustness}: Linear frequency-domain stability analysis proves an analytical delay margin of $1.58\,\mathrm{s}$ ($0.952\,\mathrm{s}$ under nominal filtering), verified against severe packet drop and latency jitter.
    \item \textbf{Guaranteed Data Center IT Reliability}: Delivering a full 30~s frequency response consumes only $0.166\,\mathrm{kWh}$ per rack, maintaining over 40 minutes of autonomous backup runtime and ensuring $100\%$ compliance with Uptime Institute Tier III/IV standards.
\end{itemize}
